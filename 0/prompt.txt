## 3. Facts from the docs

- **Ranking:** eligible first, then quality (higher is better), then cost × time (lower is better). Cost counts as at least $0.01 and time as at least 1 s (`docs/reference/atira-challenge-rfq.md:322-327`, `docs/01-challenge.md:55`). Winners are picked on the private dataset first, then the public one.
- **Quality points (bid case):** decision 5, reference 5 (also decides eligibility), discount 5, total 5, arithmetic 5, withdrawn-Q&amp;A check 5, metal-index check 5, positions 75 minus the checks. Within a position: material 20 %, quantity 15 %, base price 15 %, then 10 % each for surcharge, packaging, project bonus, final price and lead time. Each extra or duplicate position costs one position's share. No_bid case: 75 / 5 / 5 / 15 (`docs/01-challenge.md:58-80`).
- **When the clock stops:**
  - Time runs from the start of the `run` command until the answer is **accepted**: the 202 response, which for a site submission happens when the portal forwards it (`atira-llms-full.txt:1616, 1758`).
  - After a 202 the agent has 10 s to exit; that grace period is outside the measured time.
  - A malformed answer (400) does not stop the clock.
  - Setup (up to 5 min) and model warm-up don't count. The scored limit is 20 min.
  - In practice: the clock stops at the final confirmed submission, so everything after it is free.
- **Time budget** (`docs/02-architecture.md:28-39`): target ≤ 8 min in total. Brief &lt; 15 s, login &lt; 60 s, evidence &lt; 4 min, rules &lt; 30 s, submission &lt; 2 min. Missions are cut at expires_at − 6 min and the submission is forced at − 4 min. The code uses `RFQ_RUN_BUDGET` = 1080 s and `RFQ_SUBMIT_RESERVE` = 420 s.
- **Models through the proxy:**
  - Qwen3.8-Flash-Next: $0.15/M input, $0.016/M cached input, cache writes free, $0.47/M output. No usage cap; **32 simultaneous calls per team across all its runs**, further calls queue.
  - Cloudflare Clef: $0.24/M input, output not priced, 16 simultaneous calls. It is a decision model (choice / probability of yes / score), not a general LLM.
  - Your own keys for Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, xAI, Together, Fireworks are billed at published list prices. A call that reports no cost is charged at the highest published price; unpriced models outside an allowlist are refused (`atira-llms-full.txt:211-300, 1849`).
  - So Qwen is the only cheap general-purpose hosted model. The docs give no prices for the bring-your-own-key models.
- **Limits:** request body ≤ 32 MiB. At most 4 browsers (tabs and contexts are free). Up to 4 test runs at once. One submission in flight. The 32 Qwen slots are shared with teammates' test runs (`docs/08-pitfalls.md:29`); `docs/10-risks-error-handling.md:41` recommends 8–12 parallel calls per run.
- **Caching and parallel calls:**
  - The platform tip is to use prompt caching for the system prompt and tool definitions (`atira-llms-full.txt:2044`); a stable prompt prefix gets the cached price.
  - Parallel agents, browsers and calls are explicitly allowed. Hedged duplicate calls are allowed, but each one is billed.
  - Forbidden: hardcoded scenario values, pre-solving, fixed scripts, crawl-then-one-shot. Helpers may only work on pages and lists the model selected (`docs/01-challenge.md:103-112`).
